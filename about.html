// Our Story

Born from the intersection of
art and algorithm.

Prompts was founded in the heart of the GCC with a singular belief: that artificial intelligence shouldn't replace creative vision — it should amplify it. What began as a two-person experiment in AI-assisted color grading has grown into a full-service post-production powerhouse.

Today, our team spans editors, compositors, colorists, sound designers, and machine learning engineers — all working under one roof to deliver final outputs that rival the best studios on the planet. Every project we touch carries our signature blend of technical precision and emotional resonance.

// Our Process

How we bring your vision to life.

01

Ingest & Analyze

Raw footage and assets are ingested into our AI pipeline. Machine learning algorithms analyze every frame for technical quality, continuity, and creative potential.

02

AI-Assisted Edit

Our editors work alongside intelligent tools that handle rough assembly, scene detection, and sync — freeing the creative team to focus purely on storytelling.

03

Grade & Composite

Color science meets neural networks. We deliver cinema-grade color grading and seamless VFX compositing, with AI handling tedious rotoscoping and cleanup.

04

Master & Deliver

Final mastering across all deliverable formats — from theatrical DCP to social-optimized cuts — rigorously quality-checked and delivered on schedule.
