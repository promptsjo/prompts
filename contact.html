// Frequently Asked

Common questions.

What types of projects do you handle?

We work across feature films, short films, documentaries, commercials, music videos, and branded content — from single-episode edits to full series post-production.

How does AI fit into your workflow?

AI handles time-intensive tasks — automated scene detection, smart rotoscoping, object removal, and dialogue enhancement — so our creatives can focus on the art.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely. While we're headquartered in Dubai, we serve clients globally with cloud-based review platforms and real-time collaboration tools.

What's the typical turnaround time?

It depends on the scope. A branded commercial might take 5–7 business days; a feature film could span 8–12 weeks. We always provide a detailed timeline upfront.
